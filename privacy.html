1. Who we are

Tic Tac Battle (the "App") is a mobile game published by Pulsebyte ("Pulsebyte", "we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have. By installing and using the App you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please uninstall the App.

2. Information we handle

The App works offline and keeps everything below on your device. None of it is sent to Pulsebyte or to any server operated by us.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not require an account, email address, phone number or password.
  • We do not collect your precise or approximate location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or messages.
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers and we do not show advertising.
  • We do not include third-party analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or tracking tools.
  • We do not process real-money payments. Coins are earned by playing and have no cash value (see our Terms of Use).

4. How the information is used

The information described above is used only on your device, to run the game: to save and restore your progress, apply your settings and appearance, calculate rewards and rankings, and schedule the reminders you have allowed. We do not use it for profiling, advertising or any purpose unrelated to playing the game.

6. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and the App does not transmit your game data to us or to anyone else. The App is built with the Unity game engine; the App does not enable Unity analytics or advertising services.

If you tap Share with friends, the App opens your phone's standard share sheet with an invitation message and a store link. What you send, and to whom, is entirely your choice and is handled by the app you pick. If you tap Contact support or the privacy link, your email app or browser opens; those services are governed by their own privacy policies. If you download the App from Google Play, Google processes information about your installation under Google's own privacy policy.

7. Arena and other players

The Arena mode matches you against computer-controlled opponents of a similar skill level, using names and avatars generated inside the App. These opponents are not other people, so your name and results are never shared with other players. We will update this policy before introducing any feature that connects players to each other.

8. Storage, retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as the App is installed. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover it for you if you uninstall the App, clear its data or lose the device. You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the App's storage in your phone's settings (Settings → Apps → Tic Tac Battle → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the App.

9. Security

The save file lives in the App's private, sandboxed storage, which Android protects from other apps. No system connected to the internet is involved in storing it. No method of storage is perfectly secure; if your device is rooted or compromised, data on it may be at risk. Please keep your device protected with a screen lock and up-to-date software.

10. Children's privacy

Tic Tac Battle is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country). Because the App collects no personal information and has no accounts, chat, ads or online sharing of personal data, there is nothing for a child to submit to us. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will help.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the CCPA in California, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data. Since we do not hold your personal data on any server, these rights are exercised directly on your device: you can see your information inside the App, change your name in Settings, and delete everything by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. You can also withdraw your consent to notifications at any time. If you would like help or have a question about your rights, contact us using the button below.

12. International users

Because your information never leaves your device, it is not transferred across borders by us.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add a feature. The latest version is always available at this page, and the "Effective date" above shows when it last changed. Material changes will also be announced in the App or on its store page before they take effect. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.

14. Contact us

Questions, requests or concerns about this policy or your privacy? Write to Pulsebyte and we will reply as soon as we can.
