Privacy Policy

Tic Tac Battle · Effective date: 10 October 2026 · Version 1.1

The short version. Tic Tac Battle does not ask you to create an account, does not show ads, and does not include analytics or tracking tools. Your progress, settings and purchases of in-game items are stored only on your own device. The one exception is Friend rooms: if you choose to play live with a friend, your player name, avatar, level and moves pass through a secure online room to that friend for as long as the match lasts. There is no chat. We do not sell your information.

1. Who we are

Tic Tac Battle (the "App") is a mobile game published by Pulsebyte ("Pulsebyte", "we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have. By installing and using the App you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please uninstall the App.

Friend rooms (online, only when you use them). When you create or join a Friend room, the App sends the following to a room hosted on Google Firebase Realtime Database, so that you and your friend can play against each other:

InformationWhy it is sent

Like any internet service, the servers that carry the connection (Google's) necessarily see your device's IP address; we do not receive, store or use it. Room data is not linked to an account, a device identifier or your real identity.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not require an account, email address, phone number or password.
  • We do not collect your precise or approximate location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or messages.
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers and we do not show advertising.
  • We do not offer chat, voice or free-text messages between players, and we do not keep an online history of your matches.
  • We do not collect anything online when you play Classic, Battle, 2 Players, Arena, Survival or Puzzles.
  • We do not include third-party analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or tracking tools.
  • We do not process real-money payments. Coins are earned by playing and have no cash value (see our Terms of Use).

4. How the information is used

The information described above is used only on your device, to run the game: to save and restore your progress, apply your settings and appearance, calculate rewards and rankings, and schedule the reminders you have allowed. Friend room information is used only to run that live match: to show each player's name and avatar, to pass moves between the two phones, and to notice when a player leaves or loses connection. We do not use any of it for profiling, advertising or any purpose unrelated to playing the game.

6. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and the App does not transmit your game data to us or to anyone else. The App is built with the Unity game engine; the App does not enable Unity analytics or advertising services.

Google Firebase. Friend room data is stored and delivered by Google Firebase Realtime Database (Google LLC), which acts as our service provider for this one feature. Rooms are held on servers in the United States. Google processes that data under its own terms and privacy policy. We have not enabled Firebase Analytics, advertising or crash reporting.

Invitations. If you tap Share invite in a Friend room, the App opens your phone's standard share sheet with a message containing the room code and a link to a page on our website. If you tap Share with friends, it shares an invitation message and a store link in the same way. What you send, and to whom, is entirely your choice and is handled by the app you pick; the App never sees your contacts. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may record visitors' IP addresses in its server logs under GitHub's privacy statement; our pages do not use cookies or analytics.

If you tap Share with friends in other parts of the App, the same applies. If you tap Contact support or the privacy link, your email app or browser opens; those services are governed by their own privacy policies. If you download the App from Google Play, Google processes information about your installation under Google's own privacy policy.

7. Arena and Friend rooms

Arena matches you against computer-controlled opponents of a similar skill level, using names and avatars generated inside the App. These opponents are not other people, so your name and results are never shared with anyone.

Friend rooms connect you with a real person you choose to play with. Please keep in mind:

  • Your friend sees the name, avatar and level you have chosen. Pick a name you are comfortable sharing, and avoid your full real name or any contact details.
  • A room code works like a key to that one room: anyone who has the code can join (the first person to enter takes the second seat). Share it only with people you want to play with.
  • There is no chat and no way to send messages or files, so the only things another player can learn about you from the App are the items listed in section 2.
  • We do not watch rooms in real time. You can leave a room at any time, and a match ends if a player leaves or loses connection.

8. Storage, retention and deletion

Your game data stays on your device for as long as the App is installed. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover it for you if you uninstall the App, clear its data or lose the device. You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the App's storage in your phone's settings (Settings → Apps → Tic Tac Battle → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the App.

Friend room data is temporary. A room is deleted when the last player leaves it. If a phone loses connection or the App is closed unexpectedly, the leftover room can stay on the server until it is cleared out; the App ignores rooms older than 6 hours, and we clear leftover rooms from time to time. Room data is not tied to an account, so we can only find a room by its code. If you would like a room removed, email us with its code.

9. Security

The save file lives in the App's private, sandboxed storage, which Android protects from other apps. No system connected to the internet is involved in storing it. Friend room traffic is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to Google's servers, and the room database only allows access to one room at a time by its code; rooms cannot be listed or browsed. No method of storage is perfectly secure; if your device is rooted or compromised, data on it may be at risk. Please keep your device protected with a screen lock and up-to-date software.

10. Children's privacy

Tic Tac Battle is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country). Because the App collects no personal information and has no accounts, chat, ads or online sharing of personal data, there is nothing for a child to submit to us. In a Friend room the only things shared are the player name, avatar, level and moves; there is no chat, and rooms are reachable only with a code. Parents may wish to help younger players choose a name that does not reveal who they are. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will help.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the CCPA in California, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data. Since we do not hold your personal data on any server, these rights are exercised directly on your device: you can see your information inside the App, change your name in Settings, and delete everything by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. For the temporary Friend room data described above, which is not linked to your identity, you can ask us to remove a room by writing to us with its code. You can also withdraw your consent to notifications at any time. If you would like help or have a question about your rights, contact us using the button below.

12. International users

Your game data never leaves your device, so we do not transfer it across borders. Friend room data is processed on Google servers in the United States, so if you use Friend rooms from another country it will be transferred there for the length of the match.
